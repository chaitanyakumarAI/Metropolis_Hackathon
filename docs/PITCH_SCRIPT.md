# Pitch video — script (target 1:50, hard limit 2:00)

Face to camera for the intro and close; product shots in the middle. Replace bracketed parts with your real details.
Do **not** claim users, revenue or traction you don't have.

**0:00–0:15 — Who we are**
"Hi, I'm [name], a [year/program] student at [your college] and a photographer in our campus creative community.
I'm building FirstFan."

**0:15–0:40 — The problem**
"Every campus has photographers, illustrators and musicians making great work for an audience of almost nobody.
The first twenty people who show up for them are the reason they keep going — but platforms only count likes and
followers. Those early believers get no recognition, no access and no voice, and creators have no way to reward them."

**0:40–1:05 — The solution**
"FirstFan lets fans support emerging creators early on Monad. Your first support gives you a permanent rank — fan #1,
#2, #3 — that nobody can rewrite. Creators define what that's worth: founding-fan spots, tiers that unlock exclusive
work, and votes on what they make next. The contract enforces it, and your supporter passport is portable."

**1:05–1:25 — Why Monad**
"Support is small and frequent. Monad's speed and low fees make every support a real, verifiable transaction with a
note attached — so recognition is a public record, not a database row we control."

**1:25–1:45 — Go-to-market & next steps**
"We start where I already am: campus photography and art clubs. First, a pilot with [10] creators from our community
and their earliest fans. Next: real-world perks redeemed by founding status — signed prints, gig guest lists — and an
open proof-of-early-support API so venues and other apps can recognise early fans."

**1:45–1:55 — Close**
"FirstFan: be someone's first fan — and have it count. Thank you."
